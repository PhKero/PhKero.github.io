AboutDesigner by craft, pharmacist by training

I make complex medical content clear, accurate and easy to act on.

I’m a Senior Presentation & Multimedia Designer and a licensed pharmacist with six years in medical communications. I design slide kits, proposals, detail aids, SCORM courses and campaign visuals for pharmaceutical companies across Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Egypt.

Teams work with me because I read the science, not just the slides. I build from clinical studies, guidelines and SmPCs, keep every number on-brand and on-reference, and turn around medical-review comments quickly.

Scientific fluencyLicensed pharmacist: I understand mechanisms, endpoints and data before I design them.
Built for reviewBrand guidelines, references, codes and medical-review rounds are part of my process.
Fast with AIGenerative AI for 3D icons, packshots, renders and video, so high volume stays high quality.
